A · Product & location context

US-1 See the product I am dispensing

As a pharmacy technician, I want the scanning screen to name the product I am working on so I never dispense against the wrong order.

  • Header shows product name in caps, dose-type chip and the generic name beneath, with no parentheses.
  • Meta row shows NDC, source, inventory type, current door and bin, bin inventory and the med order.
  • Configured dose and dispensed dose sit in the dose bar; dispensed dose shows the running total only.
  • All of it is per product and updates when the flow advances.
US-2 Start at the right bin

As a technician, I want the flow to open on the bin holding the nearest-expiring serial so I do not have to choose a starting point.

  • Each product declares its own bin list; the first entry is the opening bin.
  • Footer shows Location n/total; single-bin products show 1/1 and the stepper is inert.
US-3 Switch to another bin deliberately

As a technician, I want a panel of every bin holding this product so I can move when the current bin cannot cover the dose.

  • Panel lists door, bin, size, expiration, inventory and the dose already taken from that bin.
  • The current bin is marked and cannot be reselected.
  • Everything already on the dispense list survives the switch.
US-4 Be told when a door unlocks or is still open

As a technician, I want door state surfaced so I am not walking to a locked cabinet or leaving one open.

  • Moving to a bin behind another door toasts "Door X is unlocked now".
  • If the current door is open, a "Door X is Open" prompt asks the user to close it first; the pending bin is remembered and applied on OK.
  • For demo purposes the prompt fires once per session, not on every switch.
  • Bin changes within the same door are silent.

B · Building the dispense list

US-5 See every serial, act only on this bin

As a technician, I want suggested items to show all locations but only allow the bin I am standing at.

  • Rows carry serial, badges, lot, expiration, source, location, inventory vials, dispense mg and volume.
  • Rows outside the current door and bin are greyed and not selectable.
  • Tapping a greyed row's action toasts the door and bin to switch to.
US-6 Add serials one at a time or in bulk

As a technician, I want both a per-row action and a multi-select so I can work at whatever pace the picking allows.

  • Each selectable row has an "Add to dispense" button that adds that serial alone.
  • Checkboxes plus "Dispense Selected" add the whole selection.
  • The header checkbox selects or clears every selectable row and shows a dash when partially selected.
  • Adds that would exceed the configured dose are refused with a toast.
US-7 Scan a serial instead of hunting for it

As a technician, I want to scan or type a serial number and have it added if it belongs here.

  • Unknown or already-dispensed serials toast "Serial not found in available inventory".
  • A serial from another bin toasts where it actually is and is not added.
  • The on-screen keyboard shrinks the scroll region rather than covering it.
US-8 Read the dispense list at a glance

As a technician, I want the list of what I have picked to stay legible as it grows.

  • Header reads "Dispense items (n)"; columns align exactly with the suggested-items table.
  • Location is one column showing Door and Bin stacked.
  • The list caps its height and scrolls internally with a sticky column header.
  • Serials from the current bin sort to the top because they are the actionable ones.
US-9 Edit anywhere, remove only what I can physically put back

As a technician, I want to correct any serial's numbers regardless of bin, but I must not remove one I cannot return to its bin right now.

  • Edit is enabled on every row in the dispense list, current bin or not.
  • Remove is enabled only for serials in the current door and bin.
  • Disabled removes are greyed; tapping one toasts "Switch to Door X · Bin Y to remove this serial" — no hover tooltips, since the device is a tablet.

C · Editing a dispensed serial

US-10 Adjust what I actually drew from a vial

As a technician, I want to edit vials, dose or volume inline when the draw does not match the label quantity.

  • Edit turns Inv, Dispense and Volume into inputs with outlined Save and Cancel actions.
  • Editing one field derives the other two from the serial's per-vial ratio — 0.5 vial = 50 mg = 5 mL.
  • Values clamp to the serial's available inventory and cannot go negative.
  • Waste (mg) is the opened vials' content minus the dispensed amount, recalculated live.
  • The dispensed-dose total follows the edited value.
US-11 Finish the edit before moving on

As a technician, I should not be able to commit a bin while a row is half-edited.

  • Both footer actions are disabled while any row is in edit mode.
  • Save or Cancel restores them.

D · Committing, skipping and unsaved work

US-12 One save per product, not per bin

As a pharmacist, I need a single atomic transaction per product — nothing is written to inventory until the whole dose is accounted for.

  • Nothing saves on a bin change; picking, editing and removing are all just local state until the dose is met.
  • Primary reads "Continue to next bin" while the dose is short — it cycles bins without saving anything.
  • Once the dispensed total equals the configured dose, the label flips to "Dispense & continue" (or "& view summary" on the last product); clicking it writes the one transaction and advances to the next product.
US-13 Never save less than the configured dose

As a pharmacist, I need the transaction to match the order exactly — there is no partial dispense.

  • The primary action stays "Continue to next bin" and never saves until the dispensed total equals the configured dose.
  • On the last bin while still short, it cycles back through the bin list rather than disabling — there is always a next step, just never a save.
  • If every bin is exhausted and the dose is still short, it toasts the shortfall instead of committing.
US-14 Skip is a whole-product decision

As a technician, since nothing saves until the full dose is met, Skip & continue is never about a bin — it always means abandoning the product.

  • Available only on a completely untouched product: nothing added, nothing edited, in any bin.
  • Checking, editing, or otherwise interacting with a serial disables it immediately, even before anything is added.
  • Removing every change or resetting the product back to its initial state re-enables it.
US-15 Confirm before abandoning a product

As a technician, I want a checkpoint before a product leaves the workflow.

  • Dialog: "Are you sure you want to skip this product?" naming the product, with NO, STAY and YES, CONTINUE.
  • Confirming marks it skipped and goes straight to the next product — no interim modal.
US-16 Switch bins freely — nothing to lose yet

As a technician, since a bin change never saves anything, I should be able to move between bins without a checkpoint.

  • Selecting another bin from the Location panel switches immediately — no save-or-discard prompt.
  • Everything picked or edited so far stays in local state and travels with the product across every bin.
  • The same applies to jumping to a different product mid-dispense.

E · Moving through the product queue

US-17 Run several products in one session

As a technician, I want the flow to carry me through every product on the order.

  • Four products are wired: Leucovorin (4 bins), Avastin (single bin), Rituximab (single bin), Paclitaxel (2 bins) — each with its own NDC, dose type, configured dose, instructions and serial pool.
  • Completing or skipping advances to the next product in list order, wrapping to earlier skipped ones only at the end.
  • Footer counter reads Product n/4.
US-18 Jump to a product from the list

As a technician, I want the product panel to show status and let me return to unfinished work.

  • Search, status filter and door filter; chips show Current, Dispensed or Skipped.
  • Dispensed products are locked — greyed, not clickable, and tapping toasts that they cannot be reopened.
  • Skipped products can be reopened, which clears their skipped status.
US-19 Know what the button will do before I press it

As a technician, I want the footer copy to describe the outcome of the click, not my position in the list.

  • "CONTINUE TO NEXT BIN" while the configured dose is still short — the word "dispense" never appears until there is something to save.
  • "DISPENSE & CONTINUE" once the dose is met, which saves and moves to the next product; "DISPENSE & VIEW SUMMARY" instead when it is the last product.
  • "SKIP & CONTINUE" flips to "SKIP & VIEW SUMMARY" on the last product, when nothing has been touched yet.
US-20 Get confirmation without a modal

As a technician, I do not want to dismiss a dialog after every product.

  • Completing a product toasts "<Product> is successfully dispensed" and moves straight on.
  • Toasts stay short — no dose or bin arithmetic.
  • Only the end of the run opens a modal: "Dispense workflow complete", with the dispensed and skipped counts.

F · Screen behaviour on a tablet

US-21 Scroll the product page as one

As a technician on a tablet, I want the whole product page to move together, with the controls I need always reachable.

  • Product header, meta, dose bar and both tables scroll as a single column.
  • The top bar and left rail never move.
  • The action bar is anchored to the bottom of the screen regardless of content length.
US-22 Touch-sized, self-explaining controls

As a technician wearing gloves, I need targets I can hit and feedback I can read without a mouse.

  • Row actions are outlined icon buttons — blue pencil for edit, red circle-minus for remove.
  • No behaviour depends on hover; every blocked action explains itself in a toast on tap.
